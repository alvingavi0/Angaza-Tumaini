The FAITH Program

Nurturing Spiritual Growth & Christ-Centered Confidence

Our Faith Program is designed to nurture deep spiritual growth and Christ-centered confidence in every child and youth we serve.
Rooted firmly in God's love, this program creates an environment where young people can discover their identity in Christ, strengthen their faith, and learn to walk with purpose.
Through a blend of intentional spiritual practices and community building activities, we guide them to grow in wisdom, character, and biblical understanding.

The faith program includes:

Daily Devotions: Creating a rhythm of Scripture reading, reflection, and prayer that grounds students in God's word each day.

Bible Studies: Age-appropriate group studies that help students understand scripture, ask meaningful questions, and learn how to apply biblical truth to everyday life.

Discipleship sessions: One-on-one and small group mentoring that focuses on spiritual formation, character development, and practical Christian living.

Worship Experiences: Time set apart for praise, worship, word, thanksgiving, and encountering God as a community.

School Outreach: Extending Christ's love beyond the center by serving local schools through ministry activities, encouragement, and leadership development.

Mentorship Programs: Connecting students with committed Christian role models who walk alongside them, offering guidance, support, and accountability.

Overall, the Faith Program seeks to build a strong spiritual foundation, equip future leaders, and empower children and youth to live out their faith boldly -both now and in generations to come.
